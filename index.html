Broad market coverage

The screener should scan as many stocks as practical.

The production goal is not to manually maintain a small ticker list. AdvisorIQ should scan a broad stock universe, remove low-quality or unreliable names, and rank the best eligible opportunities.

Phase 1

Trusted index universe

Start with S&P 500 or Nifty 500 so the first automatic picks use liquid stocks with better data quality.

Phase 2

Large and mid caps

Expand beyond the first index to capture more opportunities while still controlling liquidity and reporting quality.

Phase 3

All major exchanges

Scan as many NYSE, Nasdaq, AMEX, NSE, BSE, and other major-exchange stocks as the data provider supports.

Protection

Filter before picking

Remove missing data, poor liquidity, extreme dilution, accounting red flags, and severe financial distress before ranking.

Removal logic

Downgrade only when evidence changes.

  • Final score drops below 60.
  • Negative news is material or thesis-breaking.
  • Price breaks below the 200-day average with weak relative strength.
  • Revenue, margins, or free cash flow deteriorate.
  • Valuation becomes extreme or better alternatives rank higher.

News + geopolitical intelligence

Risk feed that explains impact, not just headlines.

Each event is classified by relevance, affected sectors, impact, and whether it changes the investment thesis.

Methodology

The scoring engine behind the MVP

AdvisorIQ is designed to be a structured research platform, not a random buy/sell signal generator.

01

Screen the universe

Filter for liquid, financially viable stocks before ranking to avoid low-quality names.

02

Score the evidence

Combine fundamentals, financial strength, valuation, growth, technicals, news, macro risk, and management quality.

03

Apply red-flag overrides

Severe governance, debt, funding, accounting, or geopolitical events can downgrade a stock even if the numeric score looks strong.

04

Explain the pick

Every selected stock gets a short “Why Picked,” main risk, best-fit investor type, and removal trigger.
